Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026 · Applies to the POIS Tracker iOS app ("the app").

POIS Tracker helps people living with post-orgasmic illness syndrome (POIS) log their symptoms, treatments and recovery, and explore what helps them. It is operated by Tiny Objects ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: tinyobjectssoftware@gmail.com.

The short version: everything you log stays on your device — your raw entries are never transmitted to us. There are no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and we never sell data. The only optional sharing is a community contribution you have to explicitly turn on, and even that sends only anonymised summary statistics (never your entries), pooled so the results describe groups of people, not you.

What the app stores — on your device only

The app keeps the following in its private database on your device. None of it is ever sent to us — not even if you opt in to community contribution, which sends only the anonymised summary statistics described further below:

  • Health and wellbeing logs. Daily wellbeing scores, symptom ratings, treatments and habits you mark as taken, personal notes, and the intimate-activity events you choose to log.
  • Personal trials. The treatment experiments you design and run in the app, including their schedule, results and your notes.
  • Profile. Sex at birth, birth year, and — optionally — height, weight and when your symptoms first appeared.
  • Account email. The email address you enter is currently stored on your device only. There is no authentication server; it exists so your account can be connected to a sync service in a future version. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.
  • Settings. Preferences such as which factors you track, reminder times, appearance, and the app-lock setting.

What ever leaves your device

  • Purchases (Apple). Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details; the app checks your subscription status locally through Apple's StoreKit. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • PDF export (you-initiated). If you use "Export for clinician", the app generates a PDF on-device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there (mail, print, files) is entirely your choice.
  • Your own device backups. Like most apps, POIS Tracker's data can be included in the device backup you control (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). Those backups are managed and encrypted by Apple under your Apple ID — we have no access to them.
  • Optional community contribution — see the next section. Off by default; nothing is transmitted without your explicit, opt-in consent, and only anonymised summary statistics are ever sent — never your raw entries.

Community: viewing vs. contributing

Viewing the Community section requires no sharing at all — the aggregate information shown there today is bundled, illustrative example data (labelled as such in the app) and reading it sends nothing anywhere.

Contributing is separate, off by default, and only available after an explicit consent screen restricted to users who confirm they are 16 or older. If you consent, the app computes and sends only summary statistics — never your raw daily logs. A contribution contains, per factor you have enough data on:

  • a relationship strength — a single number describing how a treatment or habit tracked with your wellbeing over time (a statistic, never the underlying day-by-day scores), together with the number of days it summarises;
  • a how-it-feels rating — your own sense of whether a factor helped, as one value;
  • a before/after-"O" timing read — for as-needed treatments, whether taking it before versus after was associated with a better recovery.

Each item is labelled only by the name of the factor and outcome (for example "magnesium" and "wellbeing"). Never included: your daily logs, any exact dates or timestamps, notes or free text, your profile, or any account, device, or advertising identifier.

Rate-limiting without identifying you. So that no single device can flood the pool, each contribution carries a random token the app generates for itself. The server never receives that token directly — it receives only a one-way cryptographic hash of it, which it uses solely to enforce "at most one contribution per device per week". The hash cannot be reversed to the token, is never linked to the contents of your contribution, and is never shown to anyone.

k-anonymity. A factor's community result is only ever published once several independent people (currently at least three) have contributed to it — a pooled number that describes a group is shown, never anything traceable to one person. Because each contribution carries only summary statistics for a neutrally-named factor (no name, age, sex, location, dates, or free text), a pooled result over that group cannot be traced back to any individual.

Withdrawal and deletion. At the moment you contribute, the server returns a one-time deletion token, stored on your device. Withdrawing consent in Settings stops future contributions, deletes anything still queued locally, and — if a contribution was already sent — uses that token to request its deletion from the server. Please note one inherent limit of any aggregate: a community result that was already computed and shown to others before your deletion cannot be "un-mixed" from that past result; your data is removed from all future aggregations.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or telemetry — the app contains no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising, and no sale or rental of data — there is nothing to sell; we don't have your data.
  • No tracking across apps or websites (our App Store privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false).

Security

The app's database is protected with iOS file protection (NSFileProtectionComplete), which keeps it encrypted while your device is locked. You can additionally require Face ID / Touch ID or your passcode to open the app (Settings → Privacy). Because the data lives only on your device, protecting the device itself (passcode, Find My) is the most important safeguard.

Retention and deletion

Your logged data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete a single day's entry from its day view, or use Delete account (Settings → Profile) to permanently erase everything — logs, trials, profile, settings, queued contributions and reminders — from the device. Since your logs live only on your device, this on-device deletion is complete for them. The one exception is community contributions you have already sent: those are held on our server, and deleting the app or your account requests their deletion using the one-time token on your device (subject to the aggregate limit noted in the community section). Deleting the app from your device also removes its local data (except any copies in your own backups, which you control).

Your rights and choices

  • Access / portability. Export a structured summary of your data as a PDF at any time (Settings → Clinician).
  • Rectification. Every entry can be edited or deleted in the app.
  • Erasure. "Delete account" erases everything, immediately and irreversibly.
  • Consent withdrawal. Community contribution can be withdrawn at any time in Settings; the pending queue is deleted on withdrawal.
  • EU/EEA & UK users (GDPR / UK GDPR). Because your entries are processed only on your device, we do not receive or hold your personal data; where any processing by us does occur, the legal bases are your consent (community contribution) and performance of our contract with you (providing the app). You can exercise your rights with the in-app tools above or by contacting tinyobjectssoftware@gmail.com, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Apple acts as an independent controller for purchase processing.

Children

POIS Tracker requires you to confirm you are 16 or older during onboarding, and does not allow use below that age. It is not directed at, and must not be used by, children under 16.

International transfers

Your logged data never leaves your device, so it is never transferred anywhere. If you opt in to community contribution, the anonymised summary statistics described above are sent to our community server, which may be hosted outside your country; because those contributions carry no identifier and are only ever published as pooled, k-anonymous group results, they do not identify you. Apple's processing of your purchases is governed by Apple's own terms and safeguards.

Changes

If we change this policy — in particular, before any community service goes live or any sync feature is introduced — we will update the "Last updated" date and flag material changes in the app before they take effect.
